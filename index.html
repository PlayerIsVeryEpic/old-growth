Old Growth Forests

Our In-depth



Our In-depth is about prevention of deforestation against old growth trees. Our aim with this project is to spread awareness of the importance of old growth forests, and how we can save these trees from being logged.



Why should YOU care?



Ecosystems

Old growth trees support a wide variety of species, due to their ability to cover the ground from the sun, their ability to create space for other plants, and support animals with habitats. These trees support many other species, and without them many would be unable to survive.

Climate Change

When these trees are logged, all of the carbon stored inside of these trees gets launched into the atmosphere. This causes a significant change in our climate, as these trees have been building up carbon for their entire lives. On the other hand, keeping them increases the amount of carbon then can intake, contributing further towards a cleaner environment.

Deforestation

Deforestation is a huge issue with these trees, because companies want to earn as much money as possible. Old growth forests are bigger targets for this, because of their age making them more valuable. This map shows ares which have been disturbed by companies.

Current Progress

Because of the uses of old growth trees, and to prevent companies from removing every single tree in existence, our government has directly protected 80% of old growth forests from logging companies. However, issues such as natural causes for these trees to die, and the sustainability of these trees are struggling.
