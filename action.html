Our Action

Our actions for this project are our planned Stanley Park hike, along with a lesson about old growth, and spreading awareness about importance of old growth. Read more about these below.

Stanley Park

Our Stanley Park trip featured a lesson about old growth forests, and then a hike through Stanley Park to view old growth trees. We saw several living old growth trees, as well as the Hollow Tree, and the Two Spirits Artwork. It was incredible to be so fully immersed within the forest. You could see the many generations of trees; younger ones growing from old rotting logs as the forest sustained itself.

When the Forest Breathes:
Suzanne Simard

This was a recorded event where ecologist Suzanne Simard shared her thoughts on her bestselling book, "When the Forest Breathes", highlighting the importance of old growth to her. A recording of this event is available by clicking the image.

Website

This website was built to highlight our journey, and spread awareness about old growth trees to you, the viewer. We hope that by doing this, you would like to contribute to the cause and make repairations to old growth forests possible.
