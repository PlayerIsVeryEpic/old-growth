Our Action

Our actions for this project are our planned Stanley Park hike, along with a lesson about old growth, and spreading awareness about importance of old growth. Read more about these below.

Stanley Park

Our Stanley Park trip featured a lesson about old growth forests, and then a hike around Stanley Park to view old growth forests. We visited several old growth structures, such as the Hollow Tree, and the Two Spirited Artwork.

When the Forest Breathes:
Suzanne Simard

This was a recorded event where ecologist Suzanne Simard shared her thoughts on her bestselling book, "When the Forest Breathes", highlighting the importance of old growth to her. A recording of this event is available here.

Website

This website was built to highlight our journey, and spread awareness about old growth trees to you, the viewer. We hope that by doing this, you would like to contribute to the cause and make repairations to old growth forests possible.
